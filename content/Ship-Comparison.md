# Which Ship Is Best for Saint Martin? Complete Comparison 2026–27

**কোন জাহাজে যাবেন সেন্টমার্টিন? ২০২৬–২৭ মৌসুমের জনপ্রিয় জাহাজগুলোর তুলনামূলক গাইড**

Planning a trip to Saint Martin’s Island? Choosing the right ship can make a big difference to your journey. From comfortable AC seating and private cabins to open decks and premium lounges, different ships offer different travel experiences.

But which ship is actually best for your trip?

In this guide, we compare five popular passenger vessels serving the Cox’s Bazar–Saint Martin route: MV Karnafuly Express, MV Baro Awlia, MV Teknaf, Keari Sindbad and Keari Cruise & Dine.

Whether you are travelling with family, friends, children or as a couple, this comparison will help you understand the available options before purchasing your ticket.

### A safety lesson from an earlier overcapacity incident

On 17 February 2024, a mobile court fined MV Baro Awlia ৳100,000 and MV Karnaphuli ৳30,000 for carrying passengers beyond their reported capacities. The report stated that Baro Awlia was carrying 1,070 passengers against a reported capacity of 850.

This was a historical incident, not a claim about the ships' current operations. We include it because passenger capacity and safety should always matter when choosing a vessel.

Travellers should avoid boarding beyond the permitted passenger limit and should follow the operator's and port authorities' safety instructions.

## Which Ship Is Best for Your Travel Needs?

There is no single best ship for everyone. Your choice should depend on what matters most during the journey.

### If you are travelling with family

Compare MV Karnafuly Express and MV Baro Awlia. Both have multiple seating and cabin categories. Check cabin occupancy, attached bathroom facilities, meal arrangements and the location of your seats.

### If you are travelling on a budget

Compare the Main Deck fares of Keari Sindbad with other available economy categories. Do not choose on ticket price alone; consider the seating arrangement and journey comfort.

### If you prefer a premium seating experience

Explore the lounge categories of Karnafuly Express, Baro Awlia and Keari Cruise & Dine. Confirm whether the ticket includes food, lounge access and other facilities.

### If you want a private cabin

Check MV Karnafuly Express, MV Baro Awlia and MV Teknaf for their available cabin categories. Confirm whether the quoted price is per person or per cabin.

## Important Things to Check Before Buying Your Saint Martin Ship Ticket

Before confirming your ticket, check these details:

1. **Official operating status:** Is the ship authorized and scheduled to operate on your travel date?
2. **Departure jetty:** Confirm the exact boarding location.
3. **Ticket category:** Understand whether you are purchasing a deck seat, AC seat, lounge or cabin.
4. **Cabin occupancy:** Confirm how many passengers the cabin accommodates.
5. **Total fare:** Ask whether the quoted price is one-way or round-trip.
6. **Travel pass:** Check the current government travel-pass and QR-ticket requirements.
7. **Safety:** Check the vessel's permitted passenger capacity and follow safety instructions.
8. **Cancellation policy:** Understand the operator's refund and date-change conditions.

## Historical Safety Record: Why Passenger Capacity Matters

Choosing a ship should not be based on comfort and price alone. Passenger safety, authorized capacity and proper operational arrangements are equally important.

In February 2024, Prothom Alo and The Business Standard reported that MV Baro Awlia and MV Karnaphuli were fined for carrying more passengers than their reported capacities.

The incident highlights an important point: passengers should always travel with valid tickets, follow boarding instructions and avoid overcrowded vessels.

For the latest operating status and safety-related requirements, check the relevant authorities and vessel operators.
